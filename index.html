Privacy Policy

Lavender Kids International Preschool & Daycare is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal information of families inquiring about or enrolling in our programs.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete the early registration form or request inaugural privileges, we collect:

  • Parent / Guardian full name
  • Mobile & WhatsApp contact number
  • Email address
  • Child's program / age group of interest

2. Purpose of Collection

We use this information exclusively to:

  • Issue and reserve your exclusive 35% inaugural tuition discount voucher
  • Coordinate your personal campus walkthrough and meet-the-educators session
  • Provide curriculum and enrollment timeline updates via WhatsApp, phone, or email

3. Zero Spam & Confidentiality

We respect your time and privacy. Your contact details will never be sold, leased, or distributed to third-party marketing companies.
